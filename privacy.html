Appendix · Privacy

What this site measures

Anonymous reading statistics through Google Analytics, nothing else. No accounts, no forms, no ads.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count page views and see which essays get read. It records which page you opened, how far you scrolled through an essay, which links you clicked, and the general source of your visit, such as X or a direct link. Google sets its own cookies for this.

One value in your browser

On your first visit the site saves a small note in your browser’s local storage: where you first came from, the first page you opened, and the date. It is used only to understand which channels bring readers, and leaves your browser only as part of the analytics above.

What is not collected

No names, emails, payment details or messages. There is nothing to sign up for.

Opting out

Blocking Google Analytics with a content blocker, or clearing this site’s data in your browser, stops all of the above. Questions go to @Cogit08 on X.
