Things on the desk
systems · live

An island economy under open sky. Nothing is scripted.

Sea is the outside world. Every 30 seconds the site reads one-minute BTC candles from Hyperliquid; only how unusual the last move is becomes an impulse, and a damped spring turns it into wind and waves. No price is ever shown. Nobody on the island knows the next hour.

Fish live in shoals that grow on a logistic curve: slowly when few, fast in the middle, slowly again near the limit. Storms scatter them. Fish a shoal faster than it grows back and it is gone.

Wood grows on the slopes and drifts in on the current, more after a storm. A house costs 15.

Village: every house eats from one shared store. Fishermen fill it, the horizon buys the surplus for shells. An empty store turns the lights off.

Captains: five boats, five temperaments. Halima only goes out in calm water, Rafi goes in any weather, Noor goes when everyone else stays home, Sami follows the last good catch. Same sea, different results. Storms sink boats; the lighthouse beam and the harbour protect them.

Harbour is shared by everyone who visits. Send a ship with part of the store: the cargo is your position size, the route is your horizon, the weather is the risk. In a storm the expectation turns negative and the right move is to wait.
