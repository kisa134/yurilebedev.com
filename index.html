A little about me

Trade. Build.
Think. Write.

I’m an independent trader and entrepreneur based in Dubai. Trading is my main focus. I also build software and develop game projects with collaborators.

I study and apply the First Principles Framework (FPF) in my work, with AI as part of that process. Here I want to turn private notes into clear explanations, useful questions, and things other people can inspect.

This is a personal journal, not a signal service. I’m here to show the work and have better conversations about it.

Questions

What people usually want to know first.

Is this a signal service?
No. This is a personal journal. I don’t sell signals, manage money for anyone, or tell you what to trade. Nothing here is investment advice.
What is the trading terminal?
My own terminal, built around the tools and ideas I use in daily trading. It is the main product I’m building, and I share selected decisions and development notes here.
Why build it in public?
Private notes are too easy to leave half finished. Writing for readers closes the loop and lets other people see the thinking, question it and build on it. More in essay 002.
What is the First Principles Framework?
A body of systems thinking I study and apply. I use it as a set of questions before important decisions: what exactly am I changing, who does what, is this a fact or a guess, and what would change my mind.
What are the games?
Side projects built with collaborators. Ant Kitchen is a prototype; BomberMeme is getting a standalone edition. Trading stays the main focus.
