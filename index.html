§ 04The person
behind the work

Trade. Build. Think. Write.

I’m an independent trader and entrepreneur based in Dubai. Trading is my main focus. I also build software and develop game projects with collaborators.

I study and apply the First Principles Framework (FPF) in my work, with AI as part of that process. Here I want to turn private notes into clear explanations, useful questions, and things other people can inspect.

This is a personal journal, not a signal service. I’m here to show the work and have better conversations about it.

§ 05Marginalia

Questions people ask first

Short answers. The essays have the long ones.

Q1Is this a signal service?
No. I don’t sell signals, manage money for anyone, or tell you what to trade. Nothing here is investment advice.
Q2What is the trading terminal?
My own terminal, built around the tools and ideas I use in daily trading. It is the main product I’m building.
Q3Why build it in public?
Private notes are too easy to leave half finished. Writing for readers closes the loop. More in entry 002.
Q4What is the First Principles Framework?
A body of systems thinking I study and apply, mostly as questions before decisions: what exactly am I changing, who does what, is this a fact or a guess, what would change my mind.
