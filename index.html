Things on the desk
systems · live

Four systems share the island. None of them is scripted.

Sea is the outside world. Every 30 seconds the site reads one-minute BTC candles from Hyperliquid. Only how unusual the last move is becomes an impulse; a damped spring turns it into weather. No price is ever shown.

Drift rides a current around the island, faster in a storm. Whatever reaches the coast may take root as a new node.

Colony is a network fed by the lighthouse beam. Each node stores charge; at 1 it topples and passes 80% to its neighbours. Most avalanches are tiny, a few are huge (self-organised criticality, the sandpile). A big one sends a tremor back to sea.

Swarm: signals that reach the lighthouse become fireflies. They graze the strongest nodes, so colony and swarm chase each other in loops, like predator and prey.

market ──▶ SEA ──storm──▶ COLONY ✕
SEA ──▶ DRIFT ──seed──▶ COLONY +
beam ──▶ COLONY ──avalanche──▶ SEA
COLONY ──signal──▶ ⌂ ──▶ SWARM
SWARM ──grazes──▶ COLONY −

Heat on the island marks where things just happened. Window frames use the same scale for your attention. Nothing is animated by hand: these are rules, and the shapes are what the rules produce.
